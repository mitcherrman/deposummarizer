    How it works

    1. Text is read from each page of your PDF.
    2. Each page with enough readable text is summarized in a few bullet points.
    3. Download the summary as PDF or Word and ask questions in the chat.

    Good to know

    • Summaries are AI-generated. Check them against the transcript before relying on them.
    • Page numbers are positions in the uploaded PDF, not printed transcript pages.

    Your session
