About

Page-by-page summaries of deposition transcripts

BearSummarizer summarizes legal depositions for lawyers. Upload a deposition PDF to get a concise summary of each page, then ask a chatbot for details specific to the case.

What it does

  • Page summaries. Short bullet points for each summarized page, headed by its page number in the PDF.
  • English, Spanish or both. Choose the summary language before you start.
  • Keyword focus. Optionally include or exclude topics by keyword.
  • Follow-up questions. A chatbot answers questions using excerpts from the uploaded deposition.
  • Downloads. Save the summary as a PDF or Word document, and the chat as a text transcript.

How it works

  1. UploadChoose a deposition transcript saved as a PDF.
  2. Set optionsPick the summary language and an optional keyword filter.
  3. ReviewRead the summary in the browser or download it.
  4. AskUse the chatbot to find specific details in the testimony.

Good to know

  • Summaries and chat answers are generated by a language model. Check them against the original transcript before relying on them.
  • Page numbers in the summary are positions in the uploaded PDF file, which can differ from the transcript's printed page numbers.
  • An account is optional; you can summarize without signing in.
